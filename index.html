Questions owners ask

Do I have to share my logins or account numbers?

Never. The audit runs on monthly category totals — how much you spend on fuel, supplies, ads, and so on — plus the names of the cards you carry. No logins, no account numbers, no statements, no bank access of any kind.

Does this affect my credit?

The audit itself involves no credit pull and no applications. If you later choose to apply for a card from the plan, that's a normal application you control, sequenced on the walkthrough call so timing works in your favor.

When exactly do I pay?

First — before the spending form. Click Invest $400 in the Start Your Audit section and you'll be taken to secure Stripe Checkout to make the one-time $400 investment (no sales tax). After payment, Stripe sends you to the spending form, and work begins once your spending details are in. Running the calculator is free.

How does the guarantee refund work?

The finished plan shows the modeled extra rewards per year, counted at the 1¢ cash-back value. If that number is under $800/yr, you pay nothing — the $400 is refunded in full, and you keep the plan.

Is travel value real, or marketing math?

Both numbers are shown because both are real options. Cash back at 1¢ per point is the floor you can always get. Transferring points to airline and hotel partners commonly returns 2¢–3¢ per point on the right booking — 2 to 3 times the cash value — but it depends on the program, route, and dates, so the guarantee is only ever measured at the cash floor.

How long does an audit take?

It depends — a straightforward audit with clean spending totals moves quickly, while a more in-depth plan or more back-and-forth questions take longer. There's no fixed timeline.

I already have a "good" cash-back card. Is this still worth it?

Often, yes. A flat 2% card is strong on uncategorized spend but usually loses to 3%–5% category cards on fuel, ads, telecom, and travel. Run the calculator with the 2% baseline selected — if the modeled extra is under $800/yr, the guarantee means you risk nothing by checking.
